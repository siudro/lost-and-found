How it works

FoundIt is a lost-and-found assistant that searches recorded camera footage instead of a box of lost property.

  1. Each recording is analysed for items that were put down and stayed put, and for how long nobody was near them.
  2. You describe what you lost. The assistant reads the colour, item and time from your message and searches those records.
  3. Possible matches come back with the video cued to the moment, plus anything that could not be checked.

What it can't do yet

  • It sees colour and position, not what kind of item something is. Always watch the clip.
  • Anything already in view when recording starts cannot be detected.
  • The camera has to stay still.
  • It cannot confirm that an item reached the front desk, because there are no intake records.
  • No faces are recognised and nobody is identified. Audio is never used.
